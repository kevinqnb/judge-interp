### Implemented

`src/judge_interp/clef.py` loads the pinned Clef-flash release code, refuses to score a record that would be silently truncated, builds one record per row (one true/false question per non-null field) for entity detection, and draws and balances relation subsets with the same algorithm and rng as the probe analysis (a test asserts equality). `scripts/run_experiment.py` gained the `clef_entity_row` and `clef_relation_row` tasks. `analysis/clef.py` reports smECE with a bootstrap CI, reliability diagrams, Brier skill score, AUROC and per-error-type accuracy using the existing metric code, plus size-only and permutation controls. Configs: `configs/2026-10-14-clef-{entity-main,entity-line,relation-line}-test-01.yaml`, `configs/2026-10-14-clef-{entity,relation}-smoke-01.yaml` and `configs/2026-10-14-clef-analysis-01.yaml`.
